Quick Answers

What is the Pink Help Desk?

A centralized support portal to assist students with technical and academic inquiries.

How do I access my portal?

Log in using your registered school email address and password.

Forgot your password?

Click 'Forgot Password' on the login screen to receive a secure reset link.

Can I track my tickets?

Yes, you can view the real-time status of all tickets in the 'My Tickets' section.

What is the response time?

Standard response times range from 24 to 48 hours depending on urgency.

Is the Knowledge Base public?

No, you must register and log in to view detailed solution articles.

Who sees my tickets?

Only you and authorized administrators can view your ticket contents.

How do I update my profile?

Navigate to the Profile section on the navigation bar after logging in.

What issues can I report?

Wi-Fi, enrollment, grades, tuition, and portal access problems.

Can I attach screenshots?

Future system updates will include an attachment module for files.
